# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-26

iAnguis does not collect, transmit or sell personal data. The game runs offline and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Preferences and best scores are stored locally on your device. Removing the app removes this local data, subject to your device backup settings.

The website and GitHub links open external services with their own privacy policies.
